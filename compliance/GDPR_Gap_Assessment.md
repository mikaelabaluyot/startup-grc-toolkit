# GDPR Gap Assessment

## Overview

This assessment reviews selected General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) requirements relevant to ReWire.U, a fictional digital wellbeing startup that processes personal and wellbeing-related information.

The purpose of this assessment is to identify areas where additional controls or processes may be required to better align with GDPR requirements.

---

## Assessment

| Requirement | GDPR Reference | Status | Notes |
|------------|------------|------------|------------|
| Privacy Notice | Articles 12-14 | Addressed | Users are informed about data collection and processing activities. |
| Consent Management | Article 7 | Addressed | User consent is obtained before processing personal data. |
| Access Controls | Article 32 | Addressed | Access to systems and sensitive data is restricted based on business need and least privilege principles. |
| Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) | Article 32 | Addressed | MFA is required for employee accounts and privileged access. |
| Data Retention Policy | Article 5(1)(e) | Partially Addressed | Formal retention periods and disposal procedures should be documented. |
| Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR) | Article 15 | Planned | A documented process should be established to respond to access requests. |
| Data Deletion Requests | Article 17 | Planned | Procedures should support the right to erasure where applicable. |
| Data Portability | Article 20 | Planned | Users should be able to obtain their personal data in a structured and portable format. |
| Breach Notification Process | Articles 33-34 | Planned | A documented process should be established to support breach notification obligations. |

---

## Key GDPR Principles

The following GDPR principles are particularly relevant to ReWire.U:

- Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation
- Integrity and confidentiality
- Accountability

---

## Summary

The assessment indicates that ReWire.U has established foundational security and privacy controls, including access management, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), and consent management processes.

Additional governance processes supporting data retention, data subject rights, and breach notification would improve alignment with GDPR requirements as the organization grows.

---

## Disclaimer

This assessment is a portfolio exercise based on a fictional organization and should not be considered a formal legal, regulatory, or compliance audit.
