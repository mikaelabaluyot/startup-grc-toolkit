# Risk Assessment 

Risk are assessed using a qualitative methodology.

Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact

Likelihood and Impact are scored on a scale 1-5, where:

| Score | Meaning 
| ------|---------
| 1     | Low     
| 2     | Unlikely 
| 3     | Possible
| 4     | Likely  
| 5     | Very Likely

Higher scores means higher priority for treatment.
